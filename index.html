What you watch stays private.

Celesto is a player for the IPTV subscription you already have – for Android and iOS. It provides no content of its own.

Private by design

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for; we never learn who you are.
  • Your viewing stays with you. Watch history, favorites and your lists are kept only on your device. Celesto has no server of its own that receives what you watch.
  • Sign-in details are encrypted in your phone's Keychain or Keystore, and are sent only to your own provider.
  • No analytics, no tracking tools. No statistics, no crash reporters, no profiles.
  • No ads. Celesto shows no advertising.
  • Backups only when you ask – as one password-protected file you keep wherever you like.

Your IPTV provider still sees the connections to its own servers – that is how streaming works. Celesto adds nothing to that.

Hide what you'd rather not see

Some providers use explicit artwork. Long-press any poster to hide it; Celesto shows its own artwork instead. With the parental lock (Premium) whole categories and single films or series disappear behind a PIN.

What else Celesto does

  • Xtream Codes, M3U links and files, single stream links
  • TV guide, archive (catch-up) where your provider offers it
  • Picture in picture and multi-view with up to four channels (Premium)
  • Cast to your TV: Chromecast, Google TV and Android TVs such as Xiaomi (Premium)
  • Parental lock with PIN: hide categories, films and series (Premium)
  • Eleven app languages, a step-by-step setup guide
  • Celesto Free: openly licensed and public-domain films and cartoons, free for everyone
