Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This English version is provided for convenience; the German version prevails where they differ.

This policy covers the app “Celesto IPTV Player” (“Celesto”) for Android and iOS. Celesto is a player: it contains no channels, movies or series and runs no servers of its own. The app keeps your data on your device; we, the provider, do not receive it.

2. Data kept only on your device

To work, Celesto stores on your device:

  • the access details of your IPTV provider that you enter (server address, username, password, playlist links) – encrypted in the Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android),
  • the downloaded channel and title lists – encrypted in the app's private storage,
  • the TV guide (programme titles and times),
  • favorites, recently watched channels, playback positions and your settings.

None of this is sent to the provider of Celesto. It is deleted when you delete the playlist or history in the app, or uninstall the app. A backup is only created when you ask for one, as an encrypted file stored wherever you choose.

3. Connections to your IPTV provider

Celesto connects directly to the addresses you enter or that your playlist contains (your provider's servers, TV guide links, channel logos and posters). Those servers necessarily receive your IP address, the app's identifier (User-Agent) and, where your provider requires it, your access details. We have no influence over how these third parties process data; their own privacy policies apply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, as the connection is needed for the function you use.

Celesto Free. The list of free, openly licensed films is downloaded from our website on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub receives your IP address and the User-Agent, as with any website. The free films themselves are streamed from the Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons (Wikimedia Foundation, USA) and the Blender Foundation; these receive your IP address like any website. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4. Purchases (Celesto Premium)

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details. The store only tells the app whether a valid purchase exists; this is stored on your device. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Processing by Apple or Google is governed by their privacy policies.

5. No analytics or tracking by us

Celesto contains no advertising, analytics, statistics or crash-reporting tools and creates no user account. No data about your use is sent to us or to third parties, apart from the connections described in section 3.

6. Downloading the app

When you download the app, Apple or Google process data such as your account and device details under their own responsibility. We have no influence over this.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and the right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). As we do not store personal data about you ourselves, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (deleting playlists, history and favorites). For data held by Google, Apple or your IPTV provider, please contact them.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state where you live.

8. Children

Celesto is not directed at children under 16.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
